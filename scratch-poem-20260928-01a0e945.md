# Autumn Rain

Rain threads silver through the amber boughs,
Soft drums gather on the garden stone,
Wind folds the daylight into russet shrouds,
And chimneys hum their low, familiar tone.
Puddles keep a trembling piece of sky,
Leaves sail past like letters never sent,
Dusk warms the windows as the swallows fly,
While autumn rinses summer from the scent.
